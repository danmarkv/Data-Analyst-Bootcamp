## Topic

Cleaning Data in Excel

## What I did

- What to look for in Data Cleaning:
    - Data Formatting
    - Standardization
- Usual Data Cleaning Steps:
    - Remove duplicates
        - Data -> Remove Duplicates
    - Format the data
        - Use PROPER(cell_num) to capitalize the first letter and make the rest of the words lowercase
    - Use Data -> Filter to create a filter to the data
        - Look for minor spelling errors and replace those data with the correct ones
    - Identify useless column
        - Sometimes they are very dirty
    - Look at dates and standardize them
        - Short date format is usually the standard
