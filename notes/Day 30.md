## Topic

Conditional Formatting

## What I did

- Conditional Formatting is way to easily spot patterns and trends in your data using bars, colors, and icons, to visually highlight important values.
    - Icon Sets - to have visual icons on your data to indicate upwards or downwards trend.
    - Color Sets - to apply colors to your cells based on its trend.
    - Data Bars - add colored data bar in the cells to represent its value.
    - Top/Botton Rules - to highlight cell based on your preference. Top values, bottom values, top 10%, bottom 10%, etc...
- Highlight Cells Rules - it's used the most.
    - Duplicate Values - highlights duplicate/unique values depending on your preference.
    - Text that Contains - highlights cells that contain a certain string/character.
- More Rules
    - Highlight the cells you want to add rules to first, then remove the dollar symbols so that your range isn't static, then add your rule.
