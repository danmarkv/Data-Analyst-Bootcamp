## Topic

Data Cleaning

## What I did

- Data Cleaning - is the the process of identifying and fixing issues (dirty data) in your data to ensure it's accurate, consistent and complete.
- Why do we clean data?
    - It ensures accuracy
        - Decisions based on bad data can lead to costly mistakes.
    - It improves efficiency
        - Clean data is easier to analyze and saves time.
    - It builds trust
        - Stakeholders need confidence in your data to act on insights.
- Data Cleaning Cycle
    - Importing Data -> Merging Data Sets -> Rebuilding Missing Data -> Standardization -> Normalization -> De-duplication -> Verification & Enrichment -> Exporting Data -> Importing Data

## Key takeaway

- Data Cleaning is essential because it makes the data acccrate, consistent, and complete.
